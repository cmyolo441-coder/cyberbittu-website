02 / Services

Find risk.
Fix it fast.

From a targeted test to a complete security review, each engagement is scoped around the risks that matter to your business—not a generic checklist.

Penetration Testing

Simulated real-world attacks uncover exploitable weaknesses in your web apps, APIs, and infrastructure before criminals do.

Vulnerability Assessment (VAPT)

Systematic scanning and manual verification across your network and applications, with each vulnerability ranked by real risk.

Security Audit & Compliance

Thorough audits of policies, configurations, and controls help you meet standards and close security gaps.

Incident Response

Rapid containment, investigation, and recovery when something goes wrong—so you can get back to business fast.

Network Security

Firewall reviews, segmentation checks, and hardening for the internal and cloud networks your business depends on.

Security Awareness Training

Practical training gives your team the confidence to spot phishing and social-engineering attacks before they land.
